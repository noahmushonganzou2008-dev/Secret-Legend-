Meet Secret Legend

“Music is my voice. My dream is my future. My purpose is to inspire.”

Secret Legend is a Zimbabwean country-soul artist from Chinhoyi. He started writing music in the middle of 2025 and dreams of recording and sharing his own songs with the world.

Style: Country music

Purpose: To comfort people through emotional music about love, life and more.

2026: Writing more music, including songs inspired by Christmas, New Year and life.
