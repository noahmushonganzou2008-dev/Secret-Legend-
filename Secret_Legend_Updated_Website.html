The Artist

Meet Secret Legend

“Music is my voice. My dream is my future. My purpose is to inspire.”

Secret Legend is a Zimbabwean country-music songwriter and singer from Chinhoyi. He began writing music in the middle of 2025 and dreams of recording and sharing his own songs with the world.

His purpose is to comfort people through emotional music about love, life and more.
